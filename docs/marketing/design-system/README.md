# Reason Commons design system

Working home for the look and feel of reasoncommons.com: the feel, the directions tried, and eventually the finished system. Method: the draft skill `designing-look-and-feel` in `life-itself/skills` (feel → mood board → 2–3 hero-only directions and logo on one living page → full pages → preview site). Model of a finished system: https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org (README, feel.md, brand.md, type.md, BUILD.md, specimens/, archive/). ## Feel candidates

| Phrase | Essence | Visual implication |
|---|---|---|
| Lucid instruments | Tools that make the structure of thinking visible: precise, legible, a little engineered; warmth from the people using them | Instrument panels, specimen sheets, mono with serif |
| Open workshop | Work in progress, visible and shared: versioned, open to inspection; engineering notebook rigour, commons invitation | Notebook grids, revision marks, real artefacts on the bench |
| **Precise and provisional** (lean) | Exact about what is claimed, honest that everything is "accepted for now"; the architect's working drawing | Drafting lines, revision clouds, pencil against ink |
| Civic engineering | Infrastructure for public reasoning, with the clarity of a good map or transit diagram; public-spirited, not corporate | Wayfinding, schematic diagrams, strong typographic grid |

Lean: *Precise and provisional*, with *Open workshop* as a supporting note. Draft "what we are not": AI-hype SaaS gradients; austere academic argument-mapping; wellness warmth; consultancy decks; nostalgic parchment.
