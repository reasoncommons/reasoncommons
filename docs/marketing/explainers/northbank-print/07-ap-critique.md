# Editorial and methodology critique

Proposal: `northbank-ap-complete` revision 1 by `david`. This is the author's adversarial review, not an independent reader test or Stephen Le's endorsement. The earlier script and critique remain intact as the record of the first presentation.

The new spine is substantially better. The group assesses before selecting a capacity; the mismatch is explicit; the ACT questions have actual group responses and lead to a chosen trial. The correction, workload harm and disappointing first result prevent the method becoming an advertisement. Keep the named people, the agreed endpoint and the unresolved final review.

The most serious remaining problems are claims stronger than their evidence. Calling a principle-by-principle pass a “complete assessment” would hide its unknowns. The source provides a flexible assessment framework, not a licence to certify the organization from a brief exercise. State that the working notes preserve strengths, concerns and unknowns. Put the detailed twenty-four-principle record behind the concise capacity summary in the full case and source notes, while retaining every essential inference in the main story.

The sentence asserting there is no acute uncontrolled hazard goes beyond the existing case. The team has not performed a comprehensive safety audit. It can record that its assessment identifies no immediate survival crisis, retain mandatory workshop safeguards, and explain why decision-making is its chosen starting point for this bounded issue. The source's prioritization guidance is typical, not a mechanical ranking algorithm. The group must own the judgment.

The ACT sequence now follows A, B, 1, 2, 3, 4 and C, but “Ben will stop promising prematurely” prematurely turns willingness into success. Keep the selected behaviour as a commitment to test. Private thoughts are not an outcome measure. Show how acknowledging urgency and fear can coexist with checking a feasible promise, without interpreting real production limits as psychological resistance.

“Delegation proves the business is robust” is indefensible. Five of six requests during a two-day absence give evidence about one enabling condition. They do not establish robustness against severe shocks. Preserve that distinction in the narration, figure and reassessment record.

At 1,708 words across fifty-four short paragraphs, this exceeds the skill's baseline word budget. The expansion earns its space only if assessment, capacity selection and ACT are carried by distinct drawings. Every paragraph is at most forty words before revision. Keep that ceiling close; resist putting a five-factor scorecard or seven-cell matrix on one screen. A novice should see one comparison or one matrix question at a time. Repeated stage labels provide orientation; they should not add another explanatory paragraph.

The full case needs more than a declaration that all capacities were considered. Its authored scenario record must give a dated basis, findings, unresolved questions, the choice they support, the method/format chosen, and reassessment at each consequential review. Planning possibilities over two-to-five and five-to-ten years must remain possibilities. New fictional qualitative material must be distinguished from the numerical records already present in the case.

## Ranked fixes

1. Replace broad assessment and hazard assurances with scoped judgments, mandatory constraints and visible unknowns.
2. Keep ACT commitments prospective; do not claim that naming a behaviour changes it.
3. Restrict the short-absence result to decision cover, leaving severe-disruption robustness untested.
4. Preserve the full five-factor and twenty-four-principle working record outside the main visual sequence, and show the actual evidence-to-choice bridge within it.
5. Give each ACT question and each changed interpretation its own view; keep the original trial records and thresholds fixed.

Revise these claims before storyboarding. The desired result is a complete illustrated pass through this source-defined process, with its judgments and uncertainties inspectable. It is not a real client trial, a clinical training manual or a certification of the method's effects.
