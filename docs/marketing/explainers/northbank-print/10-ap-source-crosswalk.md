# Northbank Print: source-to-demonstration crosswalk

This revision follows Stephen Le's October meta-protocol as its procedural reference. It expands an authored fictional case; it does not recover interviews that actually happened, certify a real organization or claim Stephen's endorsement. `engagement.json` owns the dated AP working records; `story.json` selects and illustrates them. The original numerical records and formal reasoning remain fixed.

## Sources and version choice

- [Adaptive Persistence: A Meta-Protocol for Cooperation, summary for Analogue Group](https://github.com/dvdgdn/persistent-systems-theory/blob/main/AP%20Summary%20Oct%2016%2C%202026%20v1.pdf), internally updated 11 October 2026 despite its Oct 16 filename: process and flexibility p. 3; Orient and Assess pp. 4–6; Analyze p. 6; Adjust and ACT pp. 6–8; Repeat and termination p. 8.
- [Adaptive Persistence v1](https://github.com/dvdgdn/persistent-systems-theory/blob/main/Adaptive%20Persistence%20v1.pdf), internally updated 30 September 2026: overview and two-part assessment p. 1; environment and collaborative principles p. 2; adaptive/robustness principles, adjustment formats and iteration p. 3.

The September overview includes geographic heterogeneity, suggests considering omission of collaborative-principle assessment under high hazardousness or volatility, and returns to analysis during iteration. The October summary repeats from Assess, makes group choice and method flexibility explicit, and gives both a broad sufficient-improvement statement and a stricter termination statement tied to all Orient criteria. Northbank retains useful geographic context, uses the October five-stage process and reassessment loop, and follows its own original all-criteria endpoint. It does not turn either source's typical priority guidance into an automatic ranking rule.

## Supporting tools and evidence boundaries

The source's meta-protocol permits different working methods. Northbank chooses ACT for group adjustment. Its causal and future-reality fragments, prospective predictions, versioned wording, correction links and named action dependencies are supporting Reason Commons practices; the source does not require every AP engagement to use them. Source commentary and labels must preserve that distinction.

All source-defined steps have a fictional example, a choice or an explicit uncertainty. This is different from resolving every organizational issue or proving every connection. A twenty-four-principle pass can retain unanswered questions; a two-day stand-in check cannot establish robustness to severe shocks; a qualifying dispatch window cannot identify a unique cause. Sensitive individual matrix sheets are not reconstructed or published.
