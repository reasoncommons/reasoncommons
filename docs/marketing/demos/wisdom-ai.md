## Source and reasoning

The [Wisdom and AI page](https://ai.lifeitself.org/) is a research agenda led by Rufus Pollock at Life Itself, part of Second Renaissance. Its governing question and four strands are preserved in `apps/reasoning-demo/commons/wisdom-ai/source-questions.json`: alignment has 22 entries, risk and pace 18, broader impacts 35 and consciousness 18. These counts include each strand root, giving 93 entries altogether. The repeated short summary is not duplicated. Text and hierarchy are unchanged from the 25 October 2026 capture apart from normalized whitespace and flattened emphasis. The companion retains the capture date and original HTML fingerprint. The complete hierarchies are readable on About under Sources and limits using the shared outline and disclosure components.

`ltp/wisdom-ai/reason-commons.yaml` remains the canonical worked reasoning. Its seven statements and four links are unchanged and remain proposed: `g-wise-ai`, `g-understand`, `g-revise`, `r-uncertainty`, `r-decisions`, `f-review`, `f-revision`; and `g-understand-necessary`, `g-revise-necessary`, `r-uncertainty-decisions`, `f-review-revision`. Source-reference metadata adapts these items to the existing publication boundary. Every statement and link is attributed to the AI and identifies its starting research question, while the source author is credited only for the questions. The source does not supply these answers, establish causation, endorse the goal or validate a review practice. The two goal conditions are necessary-condition proposals, not a sufficiency claim.

The publication registry uses projection version 4, as Gandhi does, retaining proposed standings, source references and unfinished work while excluding capture notes, receipts, local paths and private collaboration content. A narrowly scoped `web_page` source kind distinguishes inference from a research question from paraphrasing an asserted answer; missing starting questions fail normalization. Existing accepted-only and fictional-example publication checks remain in force. The source-question companion is rendered separately from the reasoning JSON, and the About page explains that boundary.
