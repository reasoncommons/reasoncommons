Rufus Pollock co-develops Reason Commons. He founded Open Knowledge Foundation and created CKAN, the open-source data portal software that has powered government data portals worldwide, including data.gov and data.gov.uk. He is the author of *The Open Revolution* and co-founded Life Itself, which hosts Reason Commons, and Second Renaissance.
