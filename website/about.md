# About

Reason Commons is a project exploring a better way for groups to **think together and turn that thinking into action**. A group lays out what it is trying to achieve, what is blocking it and why, and what to do about it, and maps evidence and discussion onto that, so it builds one shared, inspectable structure instead of talking past each other in threads that never accumulate.

We are building **AI tooling** to make this cheap enough to actually use. AI drafts the trees from material you already have, and — the part we are most excited about — folds ongoing forum discussion back into them as structured annotations, so the analysis keeps improving over time instead of scrolling away.

Underneath, the structure comes from the [Logical Thinking Process](/ltp), a forty-year-old method from the Theory of Constraints. It is how the work gets done, not something you need to learn first: we keep its rigour without requiring its vocabulary.

There is no app to sign up for. There is [a skill](/skills) you can install in Claude Code or Codex today, [an explainer series](/explainers), and a live application to one movement's strategy. Enough to judge whether it is worth adopting, joining, or funding.

## Three strands

1. **Tooling.** AI-assisted tooling for creating, maintaining and evolving a group's shared reasoning, with an annotation layer that connects ongoing discussion to it so it stays current.
2. **Community adoption.** Working with communities already engaged in complex reasoning, starting with AI safety and governance, then effective altruism, climate and social movements, to demonstrate the approach and develop the practices that make it stick.
3. **Our own community.** Trying the approach within [Second Renaissance](/explainers/second-renaissance-plain/), which Rufus co-founded, where valuable contributions currently stay fragmented.
