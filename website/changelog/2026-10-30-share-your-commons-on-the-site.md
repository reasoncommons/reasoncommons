A group using the skill can now ask it to share their commons on the [commons page](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html). The group chooses what goes public, everyone named agrees to the name they choose, and they see a preview first. The skill then opens a pull request for a maintainer to review. Shared commons appear on their own shelf under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, marked as shared by their participants. You need a GitHub account; [the skill guide](/skills#share-your-commons-on-the-site) explains each step.
