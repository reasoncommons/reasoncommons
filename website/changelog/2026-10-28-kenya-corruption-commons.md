[Corruption in Kenya, 2014](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/kenya-corruption/index.html) asks why senior officials so rarely answered for corruption. It draws on Transparency International’s Anti-Corruption Helpdesk brief of October 2014: bribery, school funds lost and billions spent without records, a commission that could not prosecute, and two dilemmas over recovering stolen money. Every statement has a page reference and describes Kenya as of 2014; the AI reconstruction has no endorsement by Transparency International or recorded trial results.
