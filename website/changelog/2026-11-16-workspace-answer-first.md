[The Gandhi 2.0 workspace](/demos/gandhi-2-0-workspace/index.html) used to make you scroll through a problem, a change and a step before you reached an action. It now opens on the goal, the action to start with and why that action comes first: nothing recorded has to happen before it, and the other seven actions come after it. Four lines underneath say how the action serves the goal, and each opens in place when you want to check it. The other views follow the same rule: question pages list their parts by title, and a statement page shows the statement before what leads to it. The record holds no judgement of which action matters most, so the page reads the order from the record's own connections and says so. The [TDI workspace](/demos/tdi-cybernetic-futures-workspace/index.html) and the private view the skill opens work the same way. Still untested with readers.
