The two plan questions in [Reason Commons in use](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html) have been redrawn. **What has to happen first?** now runs from left to right in stages, Now, Next and Later, with each step carrying the obstacle it gets past and the critical path marked when there is one: [see Re-membering's](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/re-membering/prerequisites.html). **Who does what?** reads each action as a sentence, from its starting point to the effect it should have, with who is meant to do it and the steps it delivers; where no order is recorded, a timeline places each action by those steps, as in [Gandhi 2.0](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/gandhi-2-0/actions.html). On both, a dashed card is proposed and a solid one accepted, with hollow marks for whoever proposed it and filled marks for whoever accepted it. Select a step to trace what it needs and what it makes possible: [try Willow Lane](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/example-willow-lane/prerequisites.html).
