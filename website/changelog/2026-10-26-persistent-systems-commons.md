[Why Things Last](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/persistent-systems/index.html) asks what keeps a group going while others, under the same pressures, come apart. It draws on Stephen Le’s Persistent Systems Theory and his Adaptive Persistence protocol: cooperation, adaptation and robustness matched to the environment a group actually faces, two dilemmas, the protocol’s five steps and the theory’s own warning that sorting is not understanding. Every statement has a section or page reference to the original documents; the AI reconstruction has no author endorsement or recorded trial results.
