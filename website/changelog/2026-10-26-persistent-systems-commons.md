[Persistent Systems Theory](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/persistent-systems/index.html) asks what keeps a group going while others, under the same pressures, come apart. It draws on Stephen Le’s Adaptive Persistence protocol: cooperation, adaptation and robustness matched to the environment a group actually faces, a dilemma between openness and self-protection, and the protocol’s five steps. Every statement has a page reference to the original documents; the AI reconstruction has no author endorsement or recorded trial results.
