Ask the skill to "show me our commons in the browser" and it lays out the whole working file as pages like the [demonstration commons](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html): an overview, a map, a page for each question, and every statement with who contributed it, who accepted it and what is still open. Accepted, proposed and disputed reasoning are all shown, each marked. The pages stay on your computer, beside the working file, and nothing is published or sent. See [the skill](/skills) for how to ask.
