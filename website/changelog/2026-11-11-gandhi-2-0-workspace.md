[The Gandhi 2.0 workspace](/demos/gandhi-2-0-workspace/index.html) is a prototype that reads the same record as the [Gandhi 2.0 commons](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/gandhi-2-0/index.html) in a different order. It opens on one path from the goal to a first action, and any stop on it can be swapped for another the record links there. From any statement you can trace what leads to it and what follows, and a side panel shows its exact wording, who proposed it, its sources and what is still open. The commons pages are unchanged. We have not yet tested it with readers.
