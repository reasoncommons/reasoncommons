Goal trees in [Reason Commons in use](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html) now read from the top down, with each condition indented beneath what it is needed for, so a large tree no longer runs off the side of the screen. Switch on **Step through** to start from the goal alone and open one level at a time: [try it on Reason Commons' own goal](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/reason-commons/goal.html). It starts switched on for larger trees and remembers your choice.
