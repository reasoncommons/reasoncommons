[Evolving Prosocial AI](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/prosocial-ai/index.html) asks whether AI will strengthen cooperation or extract from it. It draws on Paul Atkins and David Sloan Wilson’s forthcoming Cambridge Element: extractive worldviews that reward themselves, harms from a person’s eroded skills to the planet’s energy bill, two dilemmas, and changes that could make cooperation pay, with the authors’ own cautions alongside. Every statement has a page reference; the AI reconstruction has no author endorsement or recorded trial results.
