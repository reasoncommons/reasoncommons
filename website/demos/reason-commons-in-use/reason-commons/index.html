The change chosen

What they decided to do about it.

The change and its effects →
ChangeAccepted for nowLead now with incremental sessions, hands-on with people (J2), on a staged path (J6). Stage 1: AI-incremental with a human alongside (demos, first users). Stage 2: AI-incremental alone, perhaps with a short training video. Stage 3: AI one-shot from a curated corpus, plus an incremental walkthrough and correction. Side track: Discord enhances existing trees. Gate between stages: users act on the tree, a decision follows within 2-4 weeks, they come back, and logged human interventions fall. T2 gates Stage 2; T1 gates Stage 3.Accepted for now by Rufus Pollock and David JosephChangeAccepted for nowEvery lead pairs the named, proven method (L1) and the visible structure chat lacks (L3: trees, every claim with an author, nothing agreed until you accept) with a concrete benefit verb, not "use" (I6). Newcomers are the default audience, since TOC practitioners are rare: plain words and proof up front, tree jargon one level down (I7). The crafted AI experience is a core differentiator: how the skill walks you through, naming, beautiful well-designed trees, efficiency; method plus AI alone is not enough (I8).Accepted for now by Rufus Pollock and David Joseph
