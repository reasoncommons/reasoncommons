Explore Reason Commons

One goal, 4 questions

Choose a question to explore its reasoning, inspect a statement or bring a contribution.

The goalA reason commons is emerging and widely used: people, with AI, build shared tree-structured reasoning (such as Logical Thinking Process maps) and weave it into discussion and work, so discourse is more constructive and people find the leverage points and achieve their goals together far more effectively.
