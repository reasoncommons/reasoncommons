The goal

Every child at Willow Lane Primary can walk, scoot or cycle to school safely.

Accepted for now Accepted for now by Amara, Grace, Tomasz, Dev and Ruth

Five people with different stakes, a parent, the head teacher, the bakery owner, a council highways officer and a neighbour on the next street, work out how to make the walk to school safe without costing the shops their trade. They agreed a change, tried it, and the first reports are in.

1 · The goal

What it depends on

Four conditions are accepted as necessary for the goal. Tomasz added the fourth: the shops keep the customers they rely on.

The goal and its conditions →

2 · In the way

What stands in the way

Read from the bottom up. Parents driving to work, with nowhere to stop, together contribute to the queue at the gate. Tomasz's claim that most cars are only passing through is disputed.

All 9 statements about what stands in the way →

3 and 4 · Pulling apart, and the change

Where needs pulled two ways, and what they chose

Both needs serve the shared objective, but the positions taken to meet them clash. The change they chose breaks the assumption under that clash: close the street only at school times, with deliveries exempt.
