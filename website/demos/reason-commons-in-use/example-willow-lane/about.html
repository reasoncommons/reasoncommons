About this record

Everything in the example's working file: accepted, proposed and disputed reasoning, the assumptions and challenges on it, open questions, and reports from a trial.

This commons is invented

Willow Lane Primary, Amara, Grace, Tomasz, Dev and Ruth, and everything they say and report, are fictional. The commons was written in the skill's own file format and checked with the skill's validator, to show what a published commons can hold. Nothing here describes a real school, street or person.

What “accepted for now” means

Named people are willing to build on a statement or connection for now. It records their judgement at the time. It does not establish truth, and it does not mean everyone agrees: each item names exactly who accepted it.

Contributed is not the same as accepted

Each item records who contributed the idea and, separately, who accepted it. Ideas suggested by the AI are attributed to the AI, and only people can accept anything.

What isn't here

Nothing is held back: the example's whole working file is published, apart from how each contribution was captured.
