Flourish together.

Heath’s starting point

Becoming through relationship

Heath describes selves and capacities taking shape through ongoing relations among bodies, others, tools and environments. Agency unfolds through reciprocal activity; some uncertainty can bring forth new meanings and possibilities. These cards reconstruct her account, before the AI’s proposed practical application.

What stands in the way

When regulation becomes an individual problem

The map now connects the private-machine framing to individual optimisation, reduced collective support, pressure for predictability and technologies without shared control. Isolation, unrepaired rupture, brittleness and diminished agency are candidate effects to investigate.

The tension behind the choices

A candidate tension in this application

Protection from overwhelm and the ability to adapt are both legitimate needs. The AI’s proposed dilemma asks whether sufficient safety requires imposed individual regulation. Its usefulness for a particular group, and its fidelity to Heath’s emphasis, remain open for review.

What could change

Let interaction develop capacities and purposes

Offer practices people can adapt or leave; make one tool contestable and reversible; act, listen and retune. Reciprocal coordination may change capacities and what participants seek together. Pressure to appear calm and surveillance remain possible harms; safeguards need to work in practice.

What must become possible

Readiness belongs to a willing group

A host must agree the situation and criteria, make consent and withdrawal usable, secure time and repair arrangements, and establish actual control over the tool. These are proposed readiness conditions, not completed preparations.

How the proposal could be tried

Trace, agree, try and reconsider together

An illustrative sequence traces one activity over time and agrees an initial bounded purpose. A voluntary practice and a tool review can proceed in parallel. Participants assess benefits, harms, surprises and changes in their capacities or purposes, then continue, revise or stop. No host, trial or owner is assigned.
