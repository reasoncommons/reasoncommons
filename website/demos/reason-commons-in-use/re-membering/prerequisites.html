Part 1 of 3

Find a willing host and agree what matters

Only the absence of an agreed host is established here. The other obstacles are candidates for a host to check. A group needs to make the purpose and criteria its own.

Part 2 of 3

Make participation and control workable

Usable consent and harm response, available time and repair roles, and meaningful tool control address candidate obstacles. Preparation can happen in parallel; drawing these objectives does not establish readiness.

Part 3 of 3

Create shared evidence and revise together

For this illustrative pilot, consent, resources and tool control are readiness conditions for a shared assessment. Benefits, harms and surprises need actual experience and relevant participants’ judgements.
