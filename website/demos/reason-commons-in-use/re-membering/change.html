Part 1 of 4

Voluntary support, with a real right to leave

Shared rhythms, consent, protected solitude and participatory safeguards jointly support the proposed route to co-regulation and repair. Settling may help some people; pressure and loss of safety remain possible. No one owes the room their calm.

Part 2 of 4

Tools that people can inspect, change and stop

Visibility and privacy safeguards must be backed by actual authority to change the tool. More meaningful choice is a prediction; surveillance is a possible harmful branch and privacy protection is an untested remedy.

Part 3 of 4

How interaction can change capacities and purposes

Reciprocal adjustments may generate coordination that changes capacities, expectations and subsequent participation. Some surprise may open new meanings or purposes. Novelty can also overwhelm or harm; this is not a recommendation to increase uncertainty. The loop is an AI inference to examine in a concrete activity; timing and power remain open.

Bring support, choice and adaptability together

The three routes meet jointly in a revisable practice of being-with. The muted statements show the other parts’ outcomes; none is presented as sufficient on its own, and the joint route remains incomplete and untested.
