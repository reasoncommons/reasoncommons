Action 1 of 5

Trace one activity through time

Trace how bodies, others, tools and places shape attention, coordination and available possibilities over time. The condition is illustrative, not an observation of a host; later assessment feeds back into this inquiry.

Action 2 of 5

Agree the purpose and participation arrangements

A shared picture supports agreeing desired effects, observable criteria, consent and withdrawal. The action requires willing participants and workable arrangements, not agreement with the author’s ontology.

Action 3 of 5

Offer a small practice people can change or leave

With the purpose and participation arrangements in place, a host could offer an optional pause or check-in and a way to report strain. This can proceed alongside the tool review.

Action 4 of 5

Review one tool and enable practical choice

With the same agreed purpose, participants could inspect one tool and seek explanation, privacy protection and override. This branch does not depend on completing the shared-practice branch.

Action 5 of 5

Review changes in capacities and purposes

The illustrative branches come together to ask what helped, harmed or surprised people, and what changed in their capacities or purposes. Continue, revise or stop, including reconsidering the shared purpose. No owner, feedback provider or result is assigned.
