The concern · paraphrase

Agency was represented too narrowly through permission and adjustment. The reciprocal process through which capacities emerge needed a clearer place.

Why this changes the explanation

The revised branch proposes that reciprocal adjustment may bring forth coordination that no participant specified alone. That coordination may reshape capacities and subsequent participation.
