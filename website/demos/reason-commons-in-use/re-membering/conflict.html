Conflict 1 of 1

Can reliable safety coexist with shared control?

Both protection from overwhelm and adaptability matter. The apparent conflict rests on an assumption that safety requires imposed individual regulation. Check whether the positions concern the same decisions and whether participatory safeguards can meet both needs.
