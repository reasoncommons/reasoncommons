TDI — Cybernetic Futures · The goal

TDI helps groups shape flourishing futures together.

A working commons for how TDI could use collective inquiry and local experiments to shape flourishing futures.

Get involved ↓Explore the map →
Read the goal of record
TDI helps communities, organisations and institutions sense change, imagine futures and act together toward human and ecological flourishing within planetary boundaries. The working boundary is TDI’s network, its projects and the groups it works with.
