The countercurrent’s paradox
23 October 2026 · 2026-10-23.2+
23Oct 2026

A response to Leif Eriksson’s post 38. The goal’s six items become success factors, with selection between institutions and selection within them as conditions under the fifth. A sixth conflict sets surviving in the existing order against keeping its pressures from reshaping the institution, and a new change proposes altering the selection pressures themselves. All proposed.
