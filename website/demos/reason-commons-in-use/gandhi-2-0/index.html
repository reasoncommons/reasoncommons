Pulling apart · 6 conflicts

Where needs pull two ways

The analysis finds five conflicts in the discussion, and Leif’s countercurrent’s paradox of success adds a sixth that may lie behind four of them. The first sits behind the problem of central intervention: a cooperative needs enough capacity to survive, but concentrating authority to get it erodes the member control that made it worth building.

Success between institutions and integrity within

Surviving and gaining influence means building what the existing order rewards, but letting its pressures shape the institution erodes the values and relationships it exists for. Leif’s countercurrent’s paradox of success; it may lie behind four of the other five.

A possible change

Try conflict guidance that members can question and leave

The proposal is to test useful guidance while members retain control, access to human help and competence without the tool. It is one candidate way through the first conflict: if members can work through disagreements themselves, less authority needs to move upward. Each of the analysis’s fifteen risks comes with a remedy like the one beside it.

ChangeAccessible AI-supported conflict training and guidance, with voluntary participation, confidentiality and human support.From Leif Eriksson (post 29; essay) · related: Robert Bunge (posts 30, 19)ChangeEvaluation includes fairness, substantive agency, unresolved structural grievances and access to independent human support, not complaint reduction alone.From Leif Eriksson (essay)

The question to test

What would change our minds?

Do destructive conflict costs and unnecessary escalation fall, while fair treatment, independent competence and member influence remain? Fewer complaints or higher satisfaction would not settle it: costs may have moved elsewhere, or justified grievances may have been suppressed. A local success would still leave open whether small democratic groups gain enough to change which institutions survive.

Desired effectDestructive conflict imposes lower human and organisational costs.From Leif Eriksson (post 29; essay)Possible harmApparent resolution conceals domination: justified grievances are suppressed even when satisfaction improves.From Leif Eriksson (essay)

Choose one willing host and one function

The wider picture

One entry point into a broader programme

The map also covers reflective development, trustworthy governance, model adaptation, voice participation, federation, material and energy services, and common security, each with its own risks, first steps and tests. Conflict guidance is one possible starting point; no single global constraint has been established.
