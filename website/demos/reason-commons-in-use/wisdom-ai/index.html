A goal to review

Make informed choices, and keep them revisable.

The research agenda asks how we should develop and use AI wisely. This example proposes an operational goal and two possible necessary conditions.

A hypothesis to test

Can uncertainty leave choices poorly justified?

This is one possible problem to investigate, not a finding established by the source. Uncertainty could instead prompt restraint or better deliberation.

CauseUncertainty about AI’s effects can make development and adoption choices difficult to justify.AI inference · source questionsProblemSome AI choices may proceed without an adequate account of their consequences.AI inference · source questions

A proposed change

Revisit decisions as evidence changes.

Explicit review points could make choices revisable. The link is still a hypothesis, and authority and feasible alternatives may be needed.
