Make prosocial AI pay

Build capability, not dependence

AI that scaffolds thinking and honours stated preferences could leave people more capable and more demanding of good tools. Mixed evidence is kept as a risk.

Draws on Paul Atkins and David Sloan Wilson (document).

Outline
