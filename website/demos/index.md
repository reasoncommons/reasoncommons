# Demos & experiments

Unpolished by design. These are here to show the idea in motion and to try things out, not as finished product. For the finished, readable work, see [the series](/explainers).

## Commons you can read

[A read-only site of commons](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html), each a goal and the reasoning a group has built around it with the skill. Ours holds the 50 accepted statements and 14 accepted connections selected from Rufus and David's collaboration, with original wording, named endorsers and recorded context. Beside it, a clearly labelled fictional example, Willow Lane Primary, shows what else a commons can hold: proposed and disputed reasoning, assumptions, open questions, reports from a trial and a history of releases. Each commons has an overview, a page for each of its six questions, a view of every statement and connection, and a record of changes. The existing homepage stays in place while we explore this direction.

## Landing page candidates

[Seven candidate landing pages](/landing-lab/index.html) for Reason Commons, side by side, each introducing the project in a different way, with [evaluation results](/landing-lab/results/index.html) for the first five and a short visitor study. The two newest are [Find a Way Forward](/landing-lab/find-a-way-forward/index.html), which invites you to bring a problem that keeps coming back and follows an illustrated session from understanding it to trying something and returning with what happened, and [Disagree and Get Somewhere](/landing-lab/disagree-and-get-somewhere/index.html), which turns a disagreement between two colleagues into a question both sides can investigate.

## Claim-tree annotation demo

[The four-step vision, animated](/claim-tree-annotation-demo/index.html) — take a claim, decompose it into a tree, bring in source documents, and map evidence onto the nodes. A scripted walk-through of what the tooling is aiming at.

## Constraints dashboard

[A read-only viewer](/dashboard/index.html) over a full Logical Thinking Process analysis of one organisation's goals and constraints — goal tree, current reality, conflicts, future reality, prerequisites. Several analyses sit behind a project picker. Prototype quality; the data model is real, the interface is minimal.

## Goal Aligner

[An experiment](/demos/goal-aligner/index.html) in turning a room's scattered goals into one tree everyone can sign off on. Standalone single-page app. (Formerly at `/alignment/`.)

## A talk that performs the method

[A five-act presentation](/talk/2r-research-group/slides.html) built so the audience experiences the Logical Thinking Process before hearing its name — each act opens on a question, and the reveal maps every act back to the tree that produced it.

## Wisdom and AI

[Wisdom and AI](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/wisdom-ai/index.html) joins the existing demonstrations: four original source-question hierarchies and six proposed reasoning trees expanded from David Joseph’s analysis, with competing explanations, source references, open questions and conditional steps.

## Persistent Systems Theory

[Persistent Systems Theory](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/persistent-systems/index.html) maps Stephen Le’s Adaptive Persistence protocol: what keeps a group going, the dilemma it faces between openness and self-protection, and five steps it could take. Every statement is proposed and carries a page reference to the original documents.
